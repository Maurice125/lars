PUT STAFF PHOTOS IN THIS FOLDER
===============================

Name each photo after the person: lowercase, words joined with hyphens, ending in .jpg

  Dr. Rebecca Lars, MD   ->  rebecca-lars.jpg
  Michael Ondera         ->  michael-ondera.jpg

"Dr." at the start and anything after a comma (", MD", ", RN", ", PharmD") are left out of the file name.

Tips for good-looking photos
- Portrait orientation, head and shoulders, face in the upper half
- Roughly 800 x 1000 px (or larger), under 300 KB each so the page loads fast
- Bright, even lighting; plain or lightly blurred background works best

If a person has no photo yet, their card keeps the glowing placeholder automatically.
