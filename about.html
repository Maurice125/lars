Our Story

Built on a Simple Promise

Lars Medical Center opened its doors in 1999 with a single exam room, three physicians, and a conviction that world-class healthcare shouldn't feel cold or impersonal. What began as a small community clinic has grown into a full-service medical center — but that founding promise hasn't changed.

Today, we care for more than 40,000 patients a year across cardiology, neurology, oncology, orthopedics, pediatrics, and emergency medicine. Every department is built around the same idea: advanced technology and clinical excellence, delivered with the warmth of a doctor who actually knows your name.

We're proud to be internationally accredited, but prouder still of the relationships we've built with the families who trust us, generation after generation.

DR
Dr. Rebecca Lars, MD Founder & Chief Medical Officer
What Drives Us

Our Mission, Vision & Values

The principles that guide every decision we make, from the exam room to the boardroom.

Our Mission

To deliver exceptional, patient-centered healthcare that combines clinical excellence with genuine compassion — making advanced medicine accessible to every family we serve.

Our Vision

To be the region's most trusted healthcare institution, recognized for pioneering treatment, unwavering ethics, and a culture that puts human connection first.

Our Values

Integrity, empathy, and continuous learning. We hold ourselves accountable to the highest clinical standards while never losing sight of the person in front of us.
